# StudentHub

Collection of formulas and notes for students of various boards.

## CBSE Class 10 Mathematics Standard (041)

The [2026–27 handbook for the 2027 board examination](./CBSE_Class_10_Mathematics_Standard_2027_Handbook.md) covers all 14 NCERT chapters with explanations, formulas, solved examples, practice questions, and revision checklists.

Individual chapter files are in [`handbook/chapters/`](./handbook/chapters/), and the blueprint, unit summaries, and final revision sections are in [`handbook/sections/`](./handbook/sections/). The consolidated handbook remains the complete student-facing reference.

The handbook is an original study resource. The official CBSE curriculum and notices remain the final authority; check its assessment-source note before relying on marks allocations.
