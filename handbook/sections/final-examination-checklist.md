## Final examination checklist

- [ ] I have confirmed the current CBSE syllabus and official assessment notices.
- [ ] I can explain methods rather than only recall formulas.
- [ ] I show intermediate steps and use correct notation.
- [ ] I draw and label diagrams for geometry and trigonometry.
- [ ] I check signs, units, restrictions, and reasonableness.
- [ ] I distinguish area, length, and volume units.
- [ ] I attempt unfamiliar and application-based questions.
- [ ] I reserve time to check calculations and omitted subparts.
- [ ] I do not rely on unofficial chapter-wise predictions.
