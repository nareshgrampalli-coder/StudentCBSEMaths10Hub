# Educator review checklist

Use this checklist for an independent review of each chapter and its answer key. Review the canonical chapter in `handbook/chapters/`; the consolidated handbook is generated and should not be reviewed as a separate source.

## Review steps

- Confirm the chapter objectives accurately describe the content and align with the applicable CBSE/NCERT syllabus.
- Check every definition, formula, condition, worked calculation, proof, diagram, and answer-key result independently.
- Check assumptions, domains, signs, units, notation, rounding, and whether each result answers the stated question.
- Confirm each practice question maps to at least one stated chapter objective; record the objective mapping in the review log or issue.
- Apply the shared difficulty rubric consistently:
  - **Simple:** one direct recall, recognition, or routine substitution step.
  - **Medium:** select a method or combine related skills, usually with more than one step.
  - **Complex:** multi-step reasoning, modelling, justification, or transfer to a less familiar situation.
- Check that each keyed answer corresponds to exactly one question and includes enough reasoning to verify it.
- Note corrections with a stable question ID or heading, a concise description, and the resolution.

## Sign-off

Record reviewer name or role, relevant qualification, review date, chapter, outcome, and findings in `EDUCATOR_REVIEW_LOG.md`. A chapter is independently reviewed only after a qualified reviewer completes the checklist; automated validation and author self-review do not count as independent review.
