Quadratic equation

For ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0, the roots are:

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a

The discriminant b² − 4ac indicates whether the roots are real and distinct, equal, or non-real.

MathematicsCBSE · ICSE · State

Arithmetic progression

For first term a and common difference d, the nth term and sum of n terms are:

aₙ = a + (n − 1)d
Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d]

MathematicsCBSE · ICSE · State

Circle measures

For a circle of radius r:

Circumference = 2πr
Area = πr²

PhysicsCBSE · ICSE · State

Newton’s second law

The net force on an object equals its mass multiplied by its acceleration. Use consistent SI units.

F = ma

PhysicsCBSE · ICSE · State

Ohm’s law

At constant temperature and other physical conditions, current through a conductor is proportional to the potential difference across it.

V = IR

PhysicsCBSE · ICSE · State

Power and electrical energy

Electrical power is the rate of energy transfer. With voltage V, current I, and time t:

P = VI
E = Pt

ChemistryCBSE · ICSE · State

Amount of substance

The number of moles n in a sample is its mass divided by its molar mass.

n = mass / molar mass

ChemistryCBSE · ICSE · State

Balancing equations

A balanced chemical equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides. Change coefficients, not subscripts in a chemical formula.

BiologyCBSE · ICSE · State

Photosynthesis

In the presence of light and chlorophyll, plants use carbon dioxide and water to produce glucose and oxygen.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
